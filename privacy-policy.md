    <h2>📊 What We Collect</h2>

    <h3 style="margin-top: var(--spacing-lg);"><i class="fas fa-clock" style="color: #2DBAA6; margin-right: var(--spacing-sm);"></i>Time Tracking Data</h3>
    <p><strong>What We Collect:</strong> Time entries, categories/projects, start/stop timestamps, manual time adjustments, category customizations</p>
    <p><strong>How We Use It:</strong> Display your work hours, calculate billable time, generate reports, track productivity patterns</p>
    <p><strong>Where It's Stored:</strong> Locally on your device only. We do NOT upload your time tracking data to any servers or share your work records with third parties.</p>

    <h3 style="margin-top: var(--spacing-lg);"><i class="fas fa-chart-line" style="color: #1FA68C; margin-right: var(--spacing-sm);"></i>Usage and Analytics Data</h3>
    <p><strong>What We Collect:</strong> App usage patterns, crash reports, performance metrics, feature adoption</p>
    <p><strong>How We Use It:</strong> Improve app stability, understand valuable features, fix bugs and technical issues</p>
    <p><strong>Collection Method:</strong> Anonymous analytics (NOT linked to your identity), aggregate data only using MetricKit (Apple's privacy-respecting analytics framework)</p>

    <div style="background: #e8f4f8; border-left: 4px solid #2DBAA6; padding: 1rem; margin: 1rem 0;">
      <h4 style="margin-top: 0; color: #1A9885;">✅ Important: We Do NOT Track You</h4>
      <ul style="margin: 0.5rem 0 0 1.5rem;">
        <li>We do NOT link your data to third-party data for advertising</li>
        <li>We do NOT share your data with data brokers</li>
        <li>We do NOT track you across other apps or websites</li>
        <li>We do NOT serve any advertisements (Premium or Free)</li>
        <li>We do NOT collect your personal information (name, email, contact details)</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <p><strong>No Advertising:</strong> TimeKit does not display any advertisements. Your free tier experience is supported by our premium subscriptions, not by ads or tracking.</p>

    <h2>Notifications</h2>
    <p>TimeKit may request notification permission for:</p>
    <ul style="margin-left: var(--spacing-lg);">
      <li><strong>Break Reminders (Premium):</strong> Remind you to take breaks during long work sessions</li>
      <li><strong>Pomodoro Timer (Premium):</strong> Notify you when Pomodoro intervals complete</li>
      <li><strong>Goal Tracking (Premium):</strong> Alert you about progress towards daily/weekly goals</li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>Privacy:</strong> Notifications are generated locally on your device. No notification data is sent to external servers.</p>
    <p><strong>Optional:</strong> You can disable notifications anytime in iOS Settings → TimeKit → Notifications</p>

  <div class="card" style="margin-bottom: var(--spacing-2xl);">
    <h2>Third-Party Services</h2>

    <h3 style="margin-top: var(--spacing-lg);">Apple StoreKit (In-App Purchases)</h3>
    <p><strong>Purpose:</strong> Process Premium subscription purchases</p>
    <p><strong>Data Handled by Apple:</strong> Payment information, subscription status</p>
    <p><strong>TimeKit Access:</strong> We only receive subscription status (active/expired), no payment details</p>
    <p><strong>Privacy Policy:</strong> <a href="https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/" style="color: #2DBAA6;">Apple Privacy Policy</a></p>

    <h3 style="margin-top: var(--spacing-lg);">Apple MetricKit (Analytics)</h3>
    <p><strong>Purpose:</strong> Anonymous crash reports and performance monitoring</p>
    <p><strong>Data Collected:</strong> App crashes, hang rate, battery usage, scroll performance (all anonymous)</p>
    <p><strong>Privacy:</strong> MetricKit data is anonymous and NOT linked to your identity</p>
  </div>

  <div class="card" style="margin-bottom: var(--spacing-2xl);">
    <h2>Data Retention</h2>
    <ul style="margin-left: var(--spacing-lg);">
      <li><strong>Time Tracking Data:</strong> Stored locally until you delete the app or manually clear data</li>
      <li><strong>Categories & Projects:</strong> Stored locally until you delete them or the app</li>
      <li><strong>Reports & Exports:</strong> Stored locally until you delete them</li>
      <li><strong>Analytics Data:</strong> Retained by Apple for 90 days in aggregated form (MetricKit)</li>
      <li><strong>iCloud Data (if enabled):</strong> Retained in your iCloud account until you disable sync or delete data</li>
    </ul>
    <p style="margin-top: var(--spacing-lg); padding: 1rem; background: #FFF3CD; border-left: 4px solid #FF9500; border-radius: var(--radius-sm);">
      <strong>To delete all your data:</strong> Simply delete the TimeKit app from your device. All locally stored data will be permanently removed. If you enabled iCloud sync, you can also delete TimeKit data from Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
    </p>
